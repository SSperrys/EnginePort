Portenta AI Camera

By Riley Tillman Portenta vision shield camera is compact and delivers 20 ms inference at 30 FPS letting prosthetic compute grip adjustment without external computing.

Portenta H7 Board

Porteta H7 Board supports Wi-Fi/Bluetooth as well as TensorFlow-Lite. Boasts strong processing power while keeping power usage minimal.

On-Device vs. Cloud Inference

Cloud approach vs TinyML pipline the cloud approach was simpler but had a large latency and no network dependancy compared to on device option.

Model Training

Gathered ~500 object images under varied conditions with semi-automated tracing to train the custom model.
