Mechanical Design

The mechanical development of the prosthetic hand was led by Noah Tuitavuki, with support from David Rico and Michael Huang. The team focused on improving the wrist's functionality by increasing its Degrees of Freedom (DOF) from 1 to 3 compared to the previous year's design.

Wrist Option Comparison

Comparing U-joint option vs monopole gear

U-Joint Wrist Option

Utilizes a pulley system with springs to bring the wrist to the original position. Provides 3 DOF.

Monopole Inspired Gear Option

Utilizes a set of gears to actuate the main wrist gears in 2 DOFs.

Wrist Swap Mechanisim

Forearm swapping system to allow for simpified testing of the U-joint vs the Gear option.

Fully assembled prosthetic hand with silicone liquid metal sensors and AI camera utiliozing the U-joint wrist option.
