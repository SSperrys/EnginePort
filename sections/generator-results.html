Competition Results

The generator was 3D printed with PLA and put together with epoxy and interference fits. This generator had the shortest print time of around 15 hours compared to the average of 30 hours of my peers due to my minimal material usage while keeping optimal performance.

Enjoy the short video of my generator competing in the 8 m/s windspeed.

Overall, my generator achieved a cut in speed of 3.68 m/s and produced 10.52 W at 8 m/s and 17.83 W at 10 m/s on a resistive load bank. These results landed me in 3rd place over my peers.
