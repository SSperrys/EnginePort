Mechanical Design

Below are the CAD models and drawing sheets made in SolidWorks and Inventor

CAD Model Overview

This full-assembly CAD rendering shows how the generator will come together and was designed to be 3D-printed.

Drawing Sheet 1: Shell

The shell inner diameter was minimized to keep the stator coils close to the rotor while also minimizing PLA usage. The design allows for each coil to be removeable with a slight interference fit for testing and replacement.

Drawing Sheet 2: Stator Teeth

The stator teeth enter the slots of the shell with a removeable pole to allow connection on a power drill for compact windings.

Drawing Sheet 3: Rotor

The rotor design is a 8-pole rotor minimizing the space between each magnet to keep flux leakage minimal.

Drawing Sheet 4: Shell Caps

The caps were designed to be aerodynamic to ensure minimal interference on the blades. They were also considered to allow airflow to cool the coils in the case of overheating.
